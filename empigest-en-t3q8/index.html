What we heard on 22 October

Six sentences that explain the problem

Said by the Aprolis Empigest team in the meeting. Each one has an answer in the platform: click "see" to go straight to it.

01 · communicationThe customer sends an email and there is silence.

Often the coordinator sends the technician and the customer is left in the dark, not knowing if anyone is handling it.

02 · triageI go fishing for my own requests.

Five coordinators searching the same 200 emails for the ones that are theirs, by customer or by region.

03 · dataThe customer doesn't tell us the serial number.

With five rented machines at the same customer, the PAT only opens when the technician is standing next to the machine.

04 · measurementWe don't measure any SLA.

How long it takes to reply, to send the technician, to fix the machine: today nobody knows.

05 · knowledgeWe have four people looking for the right catalogue.

Thousands of catalogues from 94 brands, in several languages. The SharePoint agent worked and then stopped working.

06 · trustI don't want to tie the company to a pseudo-solution.

Power Automate failing and hours lost working out why. Whatever is built must be maintained and must not depend on one person.

We agreed to start with after-sales, the quickest win, and map the rest in parallel. Without losing flexibility or the personal contact with the customer.

Every breakdown gets a ticket, a reply and an owner in the first minute

A normal morning, sped up. Emails keep arriving in Outlook as they do today; the platform reads each one and only acts on breakdowns: it opens the ticket, replies to the customer and hands it to the right coordinator. Everything else stays in the inbox, as today.

        6 · REPAIR QUOTE

        What we learned on 30 October, for the next phase

        No work is done at a customer without a prior quote, and there is a team dedicated to it. In phase 1 the quote and parts requests are already recorded in the platform from the service visit; automating the quote itself is the most obvious next phase.

        Next phase
        Request›Assignment›Diagnosis on site›Quote · next phase›Repair and closing
        01When the quote is issued

        After the technician's assessment on site, when the repair falls outside the contract or the damage was caused by the customer.

        02It depends on the contract

        Full Service, M2 (preventive maintenance with parts charged separately), rental or customer-owned machine: the contract decides who pays and the priority.

        03How it is done today

        The technician fills in a digital form with what is needed; the request goes to the quotes team, who prepare it in PHC.

        04The volume

        Around 5,600 quotes a year, and more than 21,000 form responses across the lists of technical support, parts and quotes.

        05Follow-up

        Not every quote is approved, and there is no capacity for active follow-up today. A follow-up of quotes sent and not approved is part of the same phase.

        →Why it matters

        It is the same kind of work we already automate in other industries: read the request, look up the references and prices in the ERP, and leave the quote ready to be validated by a person.

        7 · CLOSING

        Close the PAT, the ticket closes, and the history is updated

        Closing is still done as today: the technician describes the work, records parts and time, and the customer signs on the phone. What changes is what happens next, without anyone copying anything.
