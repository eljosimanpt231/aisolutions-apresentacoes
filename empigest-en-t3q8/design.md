# Design Brief: Aprolis Empigest (English version)

English version of empigest-p4t7, made on 04/11/2026 for Sérgio Mateus to present the solution to the international group (IT in France and Spain), without us in the room.

## Differences from the Portuguese version
- All text in British English; demo emails translated (marked on the page).
- Voice agent removed (the "Bia calls the customer" view, the voice node in the process map, voice in the technician portal and KPIs).
- Phase 1 aligned with the written proposal of 31/10: audit and mapping + after-sales platform, 12 weeks from kick-off; purchasing and logistics (Duarte), repair quotes, invoicing and preventive maintenance moved to "Next phases". No prices on the page ("Investment and terms are set out in the written proposal").
- Repair quote view rewritten with what was learned on 30/10 (after the on-site assessment, contract types, digital form, about 5,600 quotes a year).
- Pre-filled invoice marked as next phase (PHC read-only in phase 1).
- Presenter guide (assets/js/guia.js): at each step the button to click pulses and a "click here" bubble points at it (Simulate the morning, the ticket to assign, Confirm, Send, Notify technician, Close the PAT, a machine in History); on the other screens the Next arrow is pointed at. Driven by 'slide:show' and 'demo:passo' events.

## Kept
Architecture, palette, typography, the Crown RC 5500 manual (PF15343, event code 311, p. 253), the simulations and the Diogo rules (no CTA, no case studies, light/dark toggle).
